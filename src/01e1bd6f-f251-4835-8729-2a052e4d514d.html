1. Lord, I would be like Thee, in all Thy purity; I would be wholly,
    Thine, and yield Thee what is mine. Oh may I do Thy will: Not mine, but
    Thine, fulfill; My heart, my will, I now resign; My life to be only Thine.
  2. Lord, I would be like Thee; Thy likeness is my plea; Devoted I would
    be, and serve Thee faithfully. Grant me a love so true That for Thee
    all I’d do; O light the flame of my spiritual fire; O be Thou my only desire.
  3. Lord, I would be like Thee, that all around may see Thy holiness and
    love that comes from heav’n above. O draw me close to Thee ’til all see
    Christ in me; My life I give, my all each day; O use me, dear Lord, I pray.

REFRAIN

To be like Thee is my greatest desire; O Master, here, oh here am I! O
take me and make me like Thee, blessed Lord; To be like Thee is my greatest desire.

After final chorus

To be like Thee is my greatest desire. Amen!

Karissa Hiebert lives near Lowe Farm, MB, Canada, with her parents and six of her siblings. She helps with housework and homeschooling, teaches piano, and enjoys music and writing. This particular song was inspired one Sunday morning when she took her little brother downstairs at church to rock him to sleep, and started singing to him. The words and music are an echo of her heart’s desire, to be like Christ. Karissa helps in publishing a magazine for young sisters in Christ, All Glorious Within.
