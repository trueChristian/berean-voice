Storage Shed—Office/Storage

From the beginning of our ministry in 2015, we have not had any dedicated office or storage space. Several of us were using spare space in our homes, which often has ended up intruding into our living space. At times we have found this to be somewhat challenging, but we wanted to try to be frugal with the money God gave us to work with.

Over the past year we have discussed the growing need to do something about dedicated office space and to spread the work out among more people. Towards the end of the year, we (as a board) all agreed that it was time to move forward with this need. So, we shared this need with a number of our readers and with several local churches.

The Lord has richly blessed us through the gracious financial gifts many of you have given. A number of people donated their time to build a 14 x 24 storage building for us. Several supply companies donated materials or offered a discount on products purchased for this project. Others donated time to help insulate and finish the inside of the new building. And still others helped with the wiring, lighting, and flooring. A big thank you to all who so generously helped with this project.

This building has been installed on a newly prepared gravel pad at Craig Baumans. As a family, Craigs will be taking over the mailing list, the subscription email, the ministry phone, the resource inventory, and the sending out of all requested CDs and printed literature (other than the Remnant magazine). Hopefully, we will be able to complete this transfer within the next month. This will free up Curvin Wenger’s family to have more time to dedicate to the editing and layout of printed literature, plus the discipleship part of the ministry. Please pray for us as we make this change and as we continue moving forward on other projects.

Craig Bauman—featured board member

Brother Craig was appointed treasurer at the start-up of our ministry in 2015. We are very grateful for his many hours of faithful service as he has fulfilled these responsibilities. Other than his duties as the treasurer for The Berean Voice, he spends much of his time in his wood shop, where he specializes in cabinetry and other such projects.

Craig has been married to Diane for 33 years. The Lord has blessed them with ten children, of which two are married. They currently have three grandchildren, which bring them much joy. Some over four years ago, the Baumans moved to Ohio from Wyoming. After their move to this community, they joined our church, Faith Christian Fellowship. A year or so later we divided into two churches on opposite ends of the county, since our church was filled to capacity. At that time Craigs stayed with Shining Light Christian Fellowship, the west church, where they have continued to faithfully serve the Lord.
