I’ll walk with God—Today—
Tomorrow may not be.
I’ll make the best of TIME—right now—
That God has granted me.

I’ll plan not for a life
That I might live in ease,
But only for today to live
My God alone to please.

Nor dare I think in years
Or move from leaps to bounds;
With God shut out, and self shut in
And selfish aims surround.

There are those who plan their lives,
Who give God little thought;
Who weigh themselves, not with God’s truth,
As every day they ought!
