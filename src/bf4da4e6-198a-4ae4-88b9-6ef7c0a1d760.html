“Boast not thyself,” I’m warned,
“Of what tomorrow brings;
Thy health and wealth—e’en life itself—
May all at once take wings!”

“This night, that soul of thine
May be required of thee:
And dost thou plan the New Year full
There, ‘neath some Christmas tree?”

“Wake up! Take stock, are you prepared?
Your lessons learned alright?
If God, in yet three hours, THIS DAY,
Should close your course TONIGHT?”
