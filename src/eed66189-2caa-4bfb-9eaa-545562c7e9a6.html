Arise be glad! For dawn is here!
Rejoice and sing! The night is past,
The Dayspring shineth from on high,
The grace of God to man is nigh!
Rejoice and praise the King of Kings!
Give thanks and laud, for more is here!

Arise and glorify the Lord!
Extol and praise His worthy name!
He shineth from the heav’ns in love,
And showers blessings from above,
His mercy reaches down to men-
Adore and praise our mighty Lord!

Give thanks and sing to Christ our King
And worship Him with reverent praise;
Bow down in awful, holy fear-
The Holiness of God is here;
In wondrous beauty bend the knee;
Adore th’almighty King of Kings!
