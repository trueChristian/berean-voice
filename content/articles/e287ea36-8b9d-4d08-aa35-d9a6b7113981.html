New Family Joins Charity Ministries

Three months ago, as we sat in the regular board meeting for The Remnant publication, and the Tape Ministry, some needs were laid on the table for discussion. The ministry had been growing and expanding, presenting us with several blessed challenges which demanded solutions. The practical sides of these challenges were discussed as follows:

  • We need more volunteer workers in the tape ministry.
  • As the Remnant grows, we need help in the pre-publication process.
  • The Web site is in need of much more development.
  • We need a man who has management abilities to oversee and co-ordinate all of the new personnel.
  • This manager needs to be an older man with authority so that young ladies can volunteer and all will work together appropriately.
  • It would be nice to have an older sister who could take some of the many counseling calls from sisters who need advice.

While all of these needs were on the table for consideration, someone mentioned the David Mong Family from the congregation in Bedford, PA. It was stated that Brother David had experience in management, and we all agreed that I should look into the possibility of him coming to help in the work.

So I sat together with the whole family and shared with them the needs we had in the ministry. I then asked Brother David if he had any leading to come and help us. The whole family sat as if in awe. David answered, “We have been earnestly praying as a family since April for God to use us in building His Kingdom.” The answer had come.

Consider how the Lord took care of our many needs:

  • David can handle the management needs very well.
  • David is an older man and can provide the proper authority and oversight to open the door for young ladies to help.
  • David’s wife, Elizabeth, has good computer skills, and she has a burden to counsel with sisters.
  • Their two girls, Megan and Lauren, have computer, editing, and data entry skills.
  • Alex has computer skills, and he can help in making tapes also.
  • Lorraine Wurtz, who is currently living with them, can also help in the tape room and fill in at home with some of the domestic work.

The Lord answered all the needs with one suggestion! We are grateful for the whole family and the many ways that they have all pitched in to strengthen and expand the work. They moved here from Bedford, PA about the time of Bible school, and they have been learning and serving ever since. The Remnant and Tape Ministry offices have taken on a beautiful family atmosphere.

We continue to function as a faith ministry, which has great challenges, but exciting blessings. You will find a financial statement for the year listed below. There are many opportunities that lie ahead of us, especially as we have funds to develop the new web site. All of us here thank all of you out there who have prayed for us and supported the ministry with your gifts. We have received so many letters informing us of the daily prayers that are lifted up for us. Thank you, and know this, that much thanksgiving is rising up before God because of your kind help.

The tapes continue to go out as usual—over one hundred fifty thousand of them this last year. The testimonies of changed lives because of them are more than we can share with you.

The Remnant publication has grown by one thousand five hundred copies through this last year. We have been so encouraged by the responses we received.

The new Charity Ministries web site is receiving three thousand solid visits per month, from all over the world. Many orders are being made through this new avenue, saving us much labor and phone costs.

New Tape Sets

Oh the unsearchable riches of Christ! He is a deep well of living treasures! Out of this living well, God has provided several more tape sets since the last catalog was printed. Below you will find a small description of each of them:

Set 110
THE WORD MADE FLESH

John D. Martin

This tape set contains a brief study of the Gospel of John. However hard it is to cover twenty-two chapters in five sessions, you can glean a lofty overview of the vision that the apostle John had of the record of Jesus Christ.

Set 111
YOUTH AT THE CROSSROADS

Rick Leibee

This set deals with practical issues facing youth in this generation. Music, human authority, courtship, moral purity, and death—some of the most critical crossroads for young people today—are closely examined. All of these messages are thought provoking and down to earth.

Set 112
I AM A DISCIPLE OF JESUS CHRIST

Denny Kenaston

This is an in-depth definition of true discipleship. Given in the form of a confession of faith, Brother. Denny covers sixteen clear areas of discipleship. These are drawn from teachings of the Lord Jesus Christ and applied to our present day youth. He challenges youth to shake off the stigma of Generation X and be radical disciples of Jesus. This would be a good set to use in a youth Bible study group. It will inspire lots of discussion.

Set 113
YOUNG MEN WHO PLEASED GOD

Zac Poonen

Four godly youth in the Bible are studied in this set: Joseph, David, Daniel, and Paul. Brother Zac has many deep insights from the lives of these young men. He applies these insights in many practical ways to the youth of today.

Set 114
GOD’S PLAN OF SALVATION

Denny Kenaston

As a lengthy proclamation of the doctrine of salvation, this tape set covers a three-fold purpose in its presentation. It is a foundational doctrinal teaching on biblical salvation. It can be given to a seeking soul who needs to understand how to be saved. It is also instructional for soul winners, who want a clearer understanding of how to lead a soul through to saving faith in Jesus Christ.

Set 115
THE MAKING OF A PRINCESS

Dora Esh

This tape set is for mothers, girls, and young ladies. Sister Dora is a wife and a mother of two children. She shares out of the lessons and experiences she went through as she was developing from girlhood into a young lady. This is very good, practical material for our daughters. It can provide good mother-daughter time to sit down and talk about modesty, boys, goals, and motives—things that most girls face as they mature.

Set 116
THE LIVING WORD SERIES

Keith Daniel

This is a tape set that we have wanted to put together for some time. It contains sermons by Brother Keith, which are mostly the word of God. He quotes scores of verses from the Bible, with preaching applications interspersed among them.
