Then the Jews cried out, "Let God be crucified.
Let God's blood be upon us and on our children."
Then the soldiers took God, and stripped off His clothes.
They put upon God a scarlet robe of mockery.
They pushed a crown of thorns down on God's head.
And placed a reed for a scepter, in God's right hand.
They bowed the knee before God and mocked Him.
They spit upon God's face and smote Him on the head.
And then they lead God away, and crucified Him.
Many that passed by reviled God, wagging their heads.
And there was darkness over all the land.
Then God cried out with a loud voice, "It Is Finished".
