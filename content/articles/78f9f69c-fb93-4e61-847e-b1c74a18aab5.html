Love is like a breath of heaven. 'Tis from God above.
Blessed truths to us are given, when the Lord we love.

Love has power that's victorious, even gives its life.
Love can never be contentious; love despises strife.

Love bears all and love is pattient. Love will suffer through
Faithful in each situation; love holds all things true.

Love is never hard or bitter. Love forgets the past.
And instead it binds together, to the good holds fast.

Never rude is love's behaviour; ne'er its own would seek.
Others best is love's endeavor; love is kind and meek.

All that's earthly soon is passing; it will end for sure.
God-made love is everlasting; ever 'twill endure.

Love is to lay our own life down, as Christ did for you.

In this way the Lord first loved us, therefore we love, too.

This song has a simple melody, but one person described it as “the most scriptural song I ever heard.” It is a song worthy to add to your collection.

Since I only heard the melody and never had notes or harmony, this music may not match the “official” music, if there is any out there. If anyone knows who the author is, or if any music has been published with this song, please contact me. -Mike Atnip
