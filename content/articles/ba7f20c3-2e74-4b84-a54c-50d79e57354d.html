A Prayer for All Our Supporters

Dear Heavenly Father, in Jesus name I pray for all the dear brothers and sisters who support this ministry. They have given, Lord. Give to them spiritual blessings, a good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over. Father, they are cheerful givers. Give them the promise that Paul wrote to the Corinthians. Make all grace abound toward them, that they may always have all sufficiency in all things. Let them abound with grace to every good work. They have dispersed abroad and given to the poor. Let their righteousness remain forever and increase the fruits thereof in each one of their lives.

In Jesus Christ’s Name,
The Editor
