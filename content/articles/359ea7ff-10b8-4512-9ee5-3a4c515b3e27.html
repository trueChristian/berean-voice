The pathway of the year
Is hidden and untried;
I will not fear, for at my side
Is One who cheers
Through all the years;
I do in Him confide.

Through darkness compass me,
And clouds about me fall,
They cannot harm;
Through all the days
God is my Guide, I shall not slide,
He orders all my ways.

When at my journey’s end
I reach the land of light.
I shall forget
The perils past,
And wond’ring, trace the way of grace
That brings me rest at last.
