Flee, dark night!
Joyful sunlight, drive all evil away!
World, I want no more of your honor, pride, and power.
I want to love you, Lord, alone!
Let the sun of your favor shine on my heart, since Christ
the Beloved has carried my sins away.

-Wolfgang Sailer, ca. 1500-1550
-Songbook of the Hutterian Brethren, #283
